Important health and safety note

RECLAIM is a self-help organizer, not medical care, diagnosis, or a substitute for treatment. Withdrawal from alcohol, benzodiazepines, and some other substances can be dangerous or life-threatening. Speak with a qualified healthcare professional before stopping or changing use if physical dependence is possible. For an overdose, seizure, trouble breathing, severe confusion, or other emergency, call 911 (U.S.) or your local emergency number.

A lapse does not erase progress. If a lapse happens, prioritize immediate safety, reconnect with support, and revise the plan without shame.
